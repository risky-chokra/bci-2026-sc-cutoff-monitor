TRANSPARENCY FIRST

Method & limitations

Candidate-driven. The dashboard starts empty and contains only scores entered by candidates. No generated or demo records are included.

Private on this device. IndexedDB stores scores in this browser; nothing is uploaded. Export JSON or Excel; use PDF / Print to save a report. Import JSON to restore or combine candidate records manually.

No identifying fields. The form does not ask for names, application IDs, roll numbers, phone numbers or email addresses. Avoid adding personal details to exported files.

Not a predicted cut-off. Entries may be incomplete, estimated or unverified. A median or percentile describes only the records currently stored on this device.
